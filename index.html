How it works

Built for quick decisions, not endless scanning.

The solver checks each dictionary entry against the exact letter counts in your rack. Blank tiles cover missing letters, while filters remove irrelevant matches before results appear.

Words are grouped from longest to shortest because longer plays are usually the hardest to spot. Each result includes Scrabble and Words With Friends values; scores from wildcard letters are correctly counted as zero.

Straight answers

Word unscrambler questions

How do I unscramble letters?

Enter the letters from your rack and select Find words. The solver compares your letters with its English word list, then groups matching words by length and displays their game scores.

Can I use blank tiles or wildcards?

Yes. Enter a question mark or asterisk for each blank tile. A wildcard can stand for any one letter and contributes zero points when the solver calculates a word score.

Can I filter unscrambled words?

Yes. You can require a starting letter, ending letter, contained letter sequence, or exact word length. Filters are applied before the final results are scored and sorted.

Is this an official Scrabble or Words With Friends tool?

No. This independent utility is not endorsed by or affiliated with the owners of those games. Dictionary acceptance can vary by game edition and region.
